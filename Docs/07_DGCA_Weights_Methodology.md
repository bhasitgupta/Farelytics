# DGCA Passenger Weighting Methodology

APIx weights top 7 canonical domestic routes based on DGCA annual passenger volume figures:

1. DEL–BOM (24.50%)
2. BOM–BLR (15.50%)
3. DEL–BLR (15.14%)
4. DEL–CCU (12.37%)
5. BLR–HYD (11.61%)
6. DEL–HYD (11.11%)
7. BOM–GOI (9.77%)

Weights sum to 1.0 (100%) and represent over 60% of domestic passenger capacity.
